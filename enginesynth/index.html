What is simulated

The sound and everything that moves come from one physical model running in your browser: no recordings, no samples.

Simulated, and drawn from the simulation

  • Sound: pressure waves leaving the tailpipes and the intake, from gas flow solved through the cylinders, valves and pipes.
  • Crank, pistons, valves: position and lift at true scale. Rotary engines: the housing bore, the eccentric shaft and the rotor's motion are exact; each of the three chambers glows with its own pressure and burn.
  • Gas colour in pipes: pressure relative to ambient (blue below, orange to white above; dark at ambient).
  • Header glow: temperature of the gas inside the exhaust pipes. The metal's own temperature is not simulated.
  • Cylinder glow and lamps: cylinder pressure, burn rate and gas temperature.
  • Tailpipe plume: its speed and density follow the gas velocity at the open end of the exhaust.
  • Dials: speed, torque, power, gear and road speed are the model's own numbers.

Decorative

  • Overrun flames: shown when the model cuts fuel at speed. Afterfire in the exhaust is not simulated.
  • Camera shake, floor reflection, glow, vignette.
  • Rotor flank shape: a circular arc that stays just clear of the housing, standing in for the real flank and its combustion pocket.
  • Where the pipes run and what the parts look like: lengths and diameters are the simulated ones; the routing and shapes are a drawing. The picture is stroboscopic, so the engine appears to turn slowly.
